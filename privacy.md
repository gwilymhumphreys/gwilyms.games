# Gwilym's Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

## What we collect

While the game is running, it sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider:

- A random per-player ID generated on your machine (no name, email, or account identifier)
- Gameplay events: purchases, band clears, session start/end, frame rate, idle stretches
- How you play: which buildings you look at and for how long, which upgrades were on offer and at what price when you bought something or moved on, how much you collect by hand, and how much time you spend collecting and looking at buildings
- Platform (Windows / macOS / Linux / Web), language, game version
- Your approximate location, such as your city. Our analytics provider works this out from your IP address when an event arrives.

If you send feedback through the game's feedback form, we also receive what you wrote, with your player ID, platform, language and game version.

## What we don't collect

- Your real name, email, or any account identifier
- Your IP address. Our analytics provider receives it with each event, as any server does, uses it to work out the approximate location above, and does not store it.
- Anything you type, other than feedback you choose to send
- Save file contents

## Why

To understand where players get stuck, what balance changes work, and where the game needs improvement.

## Who else sees it

We use [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy) to receive and store the events. We don't share data with anyone else.

## Opting out

The title screen has a Disable telemetry button. After clicking it, no further gameplay events leave your machine. The setting persists across sessions.

To turn telemetry back on, click the same button, which then reads Enable telemetry. Events start again the next time you launch the game.

Feedback is the one exception. If you write feedback in the game and send it, it is sent even with telemetry disabled, because you asked for it to be.

## Deleting your data

Email gwilyms.games@gmail.com - we'll remove all records tied to your player ID.

On Windows, macOS and Linux your player ID is the one line in the file `posthog_distinct_id`, in the game's data folder:

- Windows: `%APPDATA%\The Deconsecration Initiative`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/The Deconsecration Initiative`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/The Deconsecration Initiative`

Include it in your email so we can find your records.
