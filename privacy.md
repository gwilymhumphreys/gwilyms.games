# Gwilym's Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

## What we collect

The game sends nothing unless you turn telemetry on. If you do, then while the game is running it sends anonymous gameplay events to our analytics provider:

- A random per-player ID generated on your machine (no name, email, or account identifier)
- Gameplay events: purchases, band clears, session start/end, frame rate, idle stretches
- How you play: which buildings you look at and for how long, which upgrades were on offer and at what price when you bought something or moved on, how much you collect by hand, and how much time you spend collecting and looking at buildings
- Platform (Windows / macOS / Linux / Web), language, game version
- Your graphics card model, to help us find performance problems
- In the browser version, if the game stopped unexpectedly last time: the browser's error message, and whether the page was closed normally, was in the background or lost its graphics. This is kept in your browser until the game next starts, then sent and deleted

If you send feedback through the game's feedback form, we also receive what you wrote, with your player ID, platform, language and game version.

## What we don't collect

- Your real name, email, or any account identifier
- Your IP address or your location. Our analytics provider receives your IP address with each event, as any server does. It does not store the address or work out a location from it.
- Anything you type, other than feedback you choose to send
- Save file contents

Events sent before 10 November 2026 include an approximate location, such as the city, which our analytics provider worked out from the IP address. We stopped collecting it on that date.

## Why

To understand where players get stuck, what balance changes work, and where the game needs improvement.

## Who else sees it

We use [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy) to receive and store the events. We don't share data with anyone else.

## Turning it on and off

Telemetry is off until you turn it on. The first time you start or continue a game, the game asks whether it may send this data. The title screen asks the same question, with a button to enable it.

To turn it off again, click the same button, which then reads Disable telemetry. No further gameplay events leave your machine. The setting persists across sessions.

Versions of the game before 0.1.25 sent this data unless you turned it off with that button.

Feedback is the one exception. If you write feedback in the game and send it, it is sent even with telemetry off, because you asked for it to be.

## Deleting your data

Email gwilyms.games@gmail.com - we'll remove all records tied to your player ID.

On Windows, macOS and Linux your player ID is the one line in the file `posthog_distinct_id`, in the game's data folder:

- Windows: `%APPDATA%\The Deconsecration Initiative`
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/The Deconsecration Initiative`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/The Deconsecration Initiative`

Include it in your email so we can find your records.
